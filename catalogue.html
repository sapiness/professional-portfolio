LVGSA Catalogue

Case Study: Engineering a High-Conversion Product Catalogue for Industrial Plastic Manufacturing

Company:LVGSA

Goal: Create a standardized, professional master product catalogue to unify the brand’s offerings, streamline B2B procurement, and increase average order value (AOV) from industrial buyers.

Strategy: Develop a high-clarity, dual-format (Interactive PDF + Premium Print) product showcase emphasizing technical specifications, polymer material grades, and seamless RFQ (Request for Quote) integration.
