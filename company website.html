LVGSA Website Project

This project showcases the digital interface design for Legacy Ventures Group SA (LVGSA), a proud 100% black-owned, BBBEE Level 1 leader in South Africa's plastic manufacturing and injection moulding industry. The platform profiles their specialized services, product ranges (PET, HDPE, PP), and their internationally patented, water-saving Leakless Valve technology.
