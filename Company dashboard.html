Softmeet Company Dashboard

The interactive company dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the organization's performance and key metrics.

KEY ELEMENTS FOR THE DASHBOARD

1. Overview Summary

1.1 Total Responses: Total number of survey responses.

1.2 Response Rate: Percentage of responses relative to the total number of people surveyed.

1.3 Survey Duration: Time period over which the survey was conducted.

2. Demographics

  • Age
  • Gender
  • Province
  • Language

3. Employment and Financial Status Section

  • Age
  • Employment Status
  • Cell Phone Network Used: Distribution of cell phone network providers used.
  • Data Purchasing Patterns (Amount and Purchasing Frequency)
  • Wifi usage (Purchasing Frequency)
  • Airtime Top-Up Patterns (Amount and Purchasing Frequency)

4. Data and Connectivity Section

4.1 Mobile Data Availability:

  • Frequency of Not Having Mobile Data
  • Time Taken to Purchase More Data After Running Out

4.2 Activities Missed Without Data

5. App Usage and Preferences Section

  • Frequency of App Installation
  • Important Factors When Installing Apps
  • Installed Apps
