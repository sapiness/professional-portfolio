Gym Website Project

This single-page Gym website features a fully responsive layout built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It showcases custom navigation, informative service overviews, and an interactive, scrollable membership plan section engineered to provide a seamless user onboarding experience. Watch the video demo or explore the design assets on the right.

Users can navigate through the site effortlessly, with smooth scrolling and visually appealing elements that enhance engagement. The membership plan section is particularly noteworthy, allowing users to scroll through different options with ease, making it simple to compare and select the best plan for their fitness journey.
